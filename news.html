2026 Maker's Market

Shop and Support our School! We are excited to harness the entrepreneurial spirit of our William Bell Butler Lab 60 families at our second annual Maker's Market on November 10th and 11th! With the holidays quickly approaching, this is a great time to share your gifts with the school community and maybe get a little shopping out of the way, too. Proceeds from the event will help support our classroom and staff needs. Visit the Maker's Market site for full details.

Maker's Market Dates

Mark your calendar for November 10th and 11th, 2026. Join the school community for our second annual Maker's Market—a marketplace where William Bell Butler Lab 60 families share handmade goods, treats, and creative gifts with one another. With the holidays approaching, it is a great chance to shop local within our school community while supporting classroom and staff needs.

View schedules, vendor info, and updates on the Maker's Market site

Fall Clubs

Registration is open through Monday, August 31. Before- and after-school clubs begin after Labor Day. If applications exceed a club's capacity, participants will be selected by lottery rather than first come, first served. Questions about club content should be directed to the club leader.

Interested in Becoming a Vendor?

Whether you own your own small business or love to craft or create in your free time, we'd love to have you participate! Students are welcome, too. Registration closes October 30th.
